Liner notes

Works with

Spotify — app and web. YouTube Music in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave or Opera. Anything that plays sound on your PC.

You'll need

Windows 10 (1809+) or Windows 11, 64‑bit. One or more monitors, any refresh rate.

Lyrics from

Free, open lyric databases — many songs with exact per‑word timing. Fix any song's timing yourself.

Does it use my microphone? +

No. Rimlight only listens to the sound your PC is already playing. It never opens your microphone.

Will it slow down games? +

It fades away when a game or video is fullscreen, drops to 30 fps on battery and shows its own fps and CPU use in settings.

Can I keep just the light? +

Yes — "Light ring only" is the default. Lyrics, the player and every extra are optional, and each has its own switch.

How do I change the lyrics style? +

In fullscreen, drag the lyrics left or right, tap the arrows, or hold Tab to see all ten styles playing your song and pick one. The tray menu has them too.

What if a song's lyrics are early or late? +

Nudge them with a slider or the [ and ] keys. The fix is remembered for that song.
